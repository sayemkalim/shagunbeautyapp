Shagun Beauty Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Shagun Beauty respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal and business information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use the Shagun Beauty mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect the following information:

Account information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Login/account credentials

Order information

  • Products purchased
  • Delivery address
  • Order details
  • Order history

Payment information

  • Payment method
  • Transaction details
  • Payment status

Online payments are processed through Razorpay. For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, we collect and use the information necessary to process the order, arrange delivery, and collect payment upon delivery.

Device and usage information

  • Device information
  • Application usage information
  • Diagnostic information

We do not intentionally store complete payment card details such as full card numbers or CVV information on our own servers. Payment information submitted through Razorpay is processed according to Razorpay's applicable privacy and security practices.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to create and manage user accounts, process and manage orders, arrange deliveries, provide customer support, communicate order updates, process payments, maintain application security, and improve our services.

We may also use your information to communicate important service updates and, where permitted, information about products, promotions, or new inventory.

3. Payments

Shagun Beauty supports Cash on Delivery (COD) and online payments through Razorpay.

For Razorpay payments, payment information is processed by Razorpay and its authorized payment partners. Shagun Beauty does not intentionally store complete payment card details such as full card numbers, CVV, or other sensitive payment credentials on its own servers.

Razorpay and its payment partners may process payment information according to their applicable privacy policies and security practices. For Cash on Delivery orders, we may collect and use the information necessary to process the order, arrange delivery, and collect the payment upon delivery.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We may share necessary information with trusted service providers that help us operate the application, including payment processors, delivery and logistics providers, cloud hosting providers, analytics or technical service providers, and customer support services.

Information is shared only when necessary to provide the requested services, operate the application, maintain security, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide our services, maintain business and transaction records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.
